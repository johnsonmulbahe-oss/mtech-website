How to Open a Technology Store

WITH GOD WE BUILD SOLUTIONS

Simple step-by-step instructions for a new seller or technology business.

Main User GuideTechnology Marketplace

Before You Start

The M-TECH Marketplace is for technology products and technology businesses only. A new seller cannot publish products immediately. The business must first submit a store application and receive CEO approval.

Important: Applying for a store does not automatically approve the store. M-TECH reviews the application first to protect customers and maintain trust.
1

Get an Approved M-TECH User Account

Use an existing approved M-TECH account, or request access through the M-TECH user invitation process. If you are new, the CEO may provide a QR invitation. Submit your name and approved email, wait for approval, then complete account setup.

✓ You must be able to sign in before applying for a store.

2

Open the Technology Marketplace

Sign in to M-TECH, then open Technology Marketplace. Select Sell on M-TECH.

3

Enter Your Business Information

Complete the Store Application with the business/store name, business type, contact person, phone, email, business address and a clear description of the technology business.

Do not submit false business information. Incorrect or misleading information may cause rejection or suspension.
4

Submit the Store Application

Review the information, then tap Submit Store Application. The store will enter Pending status. You cannot approve your own store.

5

Wait for CEO Review and Approval

The M-TECH CEO reviews the application. When approved, the store becomes an authorized M-TECH Marketplace seller and can begin submitting technology products.

6

Add Your First Technology Product

Return to Sell on M-TECH. Select the approved store and enter product name, category, brand/model, price/currency, stock quantity, description and a product photo.

7

Submit the Product for Approval

Tap Submit Product for Approval. New product listings remain pending until M-TECH approves them.

8

Start Selling After Product Approval

Once approved, customers can find the product and submit a Request to Buy. Each order receives a reference number for tracking.

9

Use the Seller Dashboard

Manage products, prices, stock and incoming orders. Move orders through New, Confirmed, Processing, Ready, Completed or Cancelled status.

10

Share Your Products

Use the Share button or Share Marketplace. Supported phones can send the product through the normal device Share menu to installed social and messaging apps.

11

Request M-TECH Help for Faulty Gadgets or Products

Open Request Technical Help, select the approved store, identify the device and describe the hardware, software, networking, operating-system, security, storage, power, configuration or other technical problem.

12

Receive the Marketplace Technical-Support Discount

Approved online stores may be eligible for a CEO-controlled M-TECH technical-service discount. The standard amount, discount and final amount should be documented.

13

Maintain Your Store's Trust Status

  • Sell only approved technology products.
  • Keep prices and stock accurate.
  • Respond professionally to orders.
  • Do not misrepresent warranties or condition.
  • Respect customer privacy.
  • Use M-TECH Trust & Safety for disputes.

What the CEO Checks Before Approving a Store

  • The business is genuinely technology-related.
  • Business/contact details appear legitimate and complete.
  • The seller can be identified and contacted.
  • The seller agrees to M-TECH marketplace and trust rules.
  • The proposed products are appropriate for the technology marketplace.

Quick Summary

Account → Marketplace → Sell on M-TECH → Store Application → CEO Approval → Add Product → Product Approval → Receive Orders → Manage Orders → Request M-TECH Support when needed.
