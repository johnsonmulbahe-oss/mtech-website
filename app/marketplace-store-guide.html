How to Open a Technology Store

WITH GOD WE BUILD SOLUTIONS

Simple step-by-step instructions for a new seller or technology business.

Main User GuideTechnology Marketplace

Before You Start

The M-TECH Marketplace is for technology products and technology businesses only. A new seller cannot publish products immediately. The business must first submit a store application and receive CEO approval.

Important: Applying for a store does not automatically approve the store. M-TECH reviews the application first to protect customers and maintain trust.
1

Get an Approved M-TECH User Account

Use an existing approved M-TECH account, or request access through the M-TECH user invitation process. If you are new, the CEO may provide a QR invitation. Submit your name and approved email, wait for approval, then complete account setup.

✓ You must be able to sign in before applying for a store.

2

Open the Technology Marketplace

Sign in to M-TECH, then open Technology Marketplace. Select Sell on M-TECH.

You will see the store application area and, after approval, the product-listing tools.

3

Enter Your Business Information

Complete the Store Application carefully. Enter:

  • Business or store name
  • Business type, such as computer store, electronics shop or ICT supplier
  • Contact person's name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Business address/location
  • A clear description of the technology business and what it sells
Do not submit false business information. Incorrect or misleading information may cause the application to be rejected or suspended.
4

Submit the Store Application

Review the information, then tap Submit Store Application.

The store will enter Pending status. You cannot approve your own store.

5

Wait for CEO Review and Approval

The M-TECH CEO reviews the application. The CEO may approve, reject, suspend, or request clarification where necessary.

When approved, the store becomes an authorized M-TECH Marketplace seller and can begin submitting technology products.

✓ Store approval is controlled by M-TECH, not by the seller.

6

Add Your First Technology Product

Return to Sell on M-TECH. Under Add Product, select your approved store and enter the product information:

  • Product name
  • Technology category
  • Brand and model
  • Price and currency
  • Available stock quantity
  • Clear product description
  • Product photo

Only genuine technology-related products should be submitted.

7

Submit the Product for Approval

Tap Submit Product for Approval. New product listings remain pending until M-TECH approves them.

This protects buyers from unauthorized or inappropriate marketplace listings.

8

Start Selling After Product Approval

Once the product is approved, customers can find it in the Marketplace search/browse area and can submit a Request to Buy.

Each order receives a reference number for tracking.

9

Use the Seller Dashboard

Open Seller Dashboard to manage daily store activity. A seller can:

  • See approved and pending products
  • Update product price
  • Update stock quantity
  • See incoming customer orders
  • Move an order through New, Confirmed, Processing, Ready, Completed or Cancelled status
Keep stock and prices current. Accurate information increases customer confidence.
10

Share Your Products

Use the Share button on an approved product or use Share Marketplace. On supported phones, the normal device Share menu can send the product to installed apps such as WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, Messages, email and other supported apps.

Direct automatic posting to every external social network requires separate authorization from each social platform.

11

Request M-TECH Help for Faulty Gadgets or Products

If your store has a laptop, desktop, phone, router or other technology item with a problem, open Request Technical Help.

Select your approved store and describe the device and problem, including hardware, software, networking, operating system, security, data/storage, power, configuration or another technical issue.

M-TECH creates a technical-support reference so the issue can be reviewed and resolved professionally.

12

Receive the Marketplace Technical-Support Discount

Approved online stores may be eligible for a special M-TECH technical-service discount. The CEO controls the discount type/value and determines when it applies.

The standard service amount, discount and final amount should be documented on the support request so the benefit remains transparent.

13

Maintain Your Store's Trust Status

To remain in good standing:

  • Sell only approved technology products
  • Keep descriptions, prices and stock accurate
  • Respond to customer orders professionally
  • Do not misrepresent warranties or product conditions
  • Respect customer privacy
  • Use M-TECH's Trust & Safety process when disputes arise
  • Follow CEO instructions and marketplace rules

Serious violations may lead to product rejection, store suspension or loss of marketplace access.

What the CEO Checks Before Approving a Store

  • Whether the business is genuinely technology-related
  • Whether contact/business details appear legitimate and complete
  • Whether the seller can be identified and contacted
  • Whether the business agrees to M-TECH marketplace and trust rules
  • Whether product types are appropriate for the technology marketplace

The CEO may also apply a trust profile or verification status after appropriate checks.

Quick Summary

Account → Marketplace → Sell on M-TECH → Store Application → CEO Approval → Add Product → Product Approval → Receive Orders → Manage Orders → Request M-TECH Support when needed.
