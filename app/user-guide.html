1. Getting Started

Open M-TECH: Use the official M-TECH link, QR code, or installed home-screen app.
Install on iPhone/iPad: Open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
Install on Android: Open in Chrome → menu → Install app / Add to Home screen.
Automatic updates: Installed devices use the same live web application. Normal updates do not require a new customer link or new QR code.
If an older version remains visible after an update, close the app completely and reopen it once while connected to the internet.

2. Customer Request Form

Choose the main M-TECH service category, then choose the exact service/problem from the dropdown.
Add a short written description. Add photos, voice description or short video when useful.
Enter your name and reachable phone/WhatsApp number, choose how M-TECH should assist, then tap Submit Request once.
After successful submission, keep the M-TECH request reference number shown on the Thank You page.

3. Management Dashboard

Review new customer requests, open jobs, pending quotations, unpaid invoices, active staff and upcoming appointments.
Use the application Back control to return to the previous M-TECH screen instead of accidentally leaving the installed app.
Use Refresh only when you need the newest data immediately. The app progressively loads modules to remain responsive.

4. Customer Requests

Open a request to review customer details, exact problem, attachments and status.
Move the request through the appropriate workflow: New → Accepted → Quoted / In Progress → Completed → Closed.
Create a quotation or work order from the customer request instead of re-entering the same information manually.

5. Jobs / Work Orders

Create jobs from customer requests whenever possible. Manual jobs should only be used when there is no originating request.
Assign technicians/staff, record costs and activity, update job status, and preserve the activity timeline.
Use before/after evidence and completion information where available before closing the job.

6. Quotations, Invoices & Payments

Prepare quotations from confirmed customer requirements. Check currency, totals, validity date and customer information before sharing.
Convert approved work into invoices. Record payments against the correct invoice and preserve the remaining balance.
Use the generated public document link for professional printing/sharing rather than screenshots.

7. Customers, Organizations, Staff & Leadership

Keep one clean customer/organization record whenever possible to reduce duplicates.
Staff & Leadership profiles may include profile photo, role/title, department, employment type, status, contact details, qualifications, documents, compensation, schedule and authorized notes.
Profile photos are kept in private staff storage and are not automatically exposed publicly.

8. Appointments, Reminders, Expenses & Assets

Use appointments for customer visits, installations, meetings and training. Use reminders for follow-ups and deadlines.
Record business expenses with correct date, description, category, currency and amount.
Record M-TECH assets and maintain their status rather than mixing durable assets with ordinary expenses.

9. Services & System Settings

The CEO/Admin can edit company identity, service categories, exact sub-services, pricing/packages, business defaults, documents, customer portal settings and system preferences.
Use Fresh Start only after reading all warnings. It is intended for controlled operational reset, not normal housekeeping.
Sensitive settings, financial controls and security should remain CEO/Admin controlled.

10. QR Sharing & Distribution

Use the Sharing QR area to open, print, download and share the customer request QR/poster.
When distributing posters at schools, churches, businesses or public locations, register the location, quantity, placement and date in the QR Distribution Register.
The permanent QR/link can continue receiving application updates without reprinting every time, provided the destination URL remains unchanged.

11. Problem Solving, Innovation & Community Hub

Solve a Problem: create a structured workspace for a real challenge.
Innovation Lab: move an idea through problem definition, research, proposed solution, prototype, testing and implementation.
M-TECH Community: collaborate with approved users while keeping private company management data separate.
Knowledge Bank: preserve approved solutions so future M-TECH work can reuse what has already been learned.

12. Skills & Learning Center

Open a full M-TECH course handbook for complete learning material or use the Quick Study Summary for shorter revision.
Available learning material can be printed/saved and shared according to the controls shown in the course viewer.
Use learning paths, projects and Innovation Lab work together to build practical skills rather than only reading theory.

13. Device & Connectivity Guidance

The live system is designed for iPhone, Android, tablets, laptops and desktop browsers.
If login says Failed to fetch, the browser could not reach the authentication service. Check internet, VPN/proxy, extensions, DNS/firewall, then retry.
If a button appears stuck, wait for the page to finish loading, tap once, confirm connectivity, then capture a screenshot if the same issue continues.

14. CEO Security & Administration

Never share the CEO password. Create separate authorized accounts for staff or Hub users.
Use role-based access, audit history, account activation/suspension and emergency lock controls where appropriate.
Keep customer and staff information private and only expose information publicly when there is a clear business reason.
