How enforcement works

1. Warning
First or lower-level confirmed violations normally receive a formal warning and compliance points.
2. Temporary Suspension
Repeated or serious violations may disable the store for a CEO-defined period.
3. Permanent Ban
Continued misconduct or critical violations may lead to permanent removal after CEO review.
Appeal & Review
Store owners may submit an appeal so enforcement decisions remain documented and reviewable.
